Simple guide — what YOU need to do outside the website code
===========================================================

This file is NOT a website page. It is a to-do list for Google tools.
Your shop address and phone on Contact / Footer are already enough for visitors.


1) Keep your business details the SAME everywhere
   Name:    My Duke
   Address: Sukhrali near Mata wali Gali, Sector 17C, Gurugram, Haryana
   Phone:   same number as on your website
   Website: https://mydukepetsolution.com

   Why: Google trusts you more when the name/address/phone match on
   website, Google Maps, WhatsApp, and directories.


2) Google Search Console (tells Google “this is my site — please index it”)
   a. Open: https://search.google.com/search-console
   b. Add your site: https://mydukepetsolution.com
   c. Prove you own it (Google will show one method):
      - HTML tag: put the code Google gives into client/.env as
        VITE_GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION=... then rebuild/redeploy
      - OR download Google’s small HTML file and put it in client/public/
   d. After verification, open Sitemaps and submit:
      https://mydukepetsolution.com/sitemap.xml
   e. Optionally ask Google to index Home, /pets, /contact

   Why: Without this, Google may take longer to find your pages.


3) Google Business Profile (makes you show on Google Maps / “near me”)
   a. Open: https://business.google.com
   b. Create a listing for My Duke at your Gurugram address
   c. Choose category like “Pet store”
   d. Add photos of shop/pets, ask customers for reviews

   Why: People searching “pet shop near me” or “pets Gurgaon” often
   see Maps results first — this is separate from your website pages.
